What's inside the phone in your drawer

A phone is about 200 grams of glass, metal and plastic layered together. Scroll to take one apart.

  1. Sealed togetherGlued layers make it hard to separate by hand, which is why informal yards burn or crush phones.
  2. Taken apart properlyAn authorised dismantler opens each layer and sorts it for the right recovery process.
  3. Every layer has a destinationMetals go to smelters, the battery to a battery recycler, plastics and glass to their own streams.
  • Display glass Glass, indium tin oxide touch layer
  • Frame Aluminium, re-smelted
  • Mainboard Copper, gold, silver, palladium, lead solder
  • Battery Lithium, cobalt, nickel
  • Camera & speakers Rare-earth magnets
  • Back cover Glass or plastic

    Questions people ask before booking

    Is the pickup really free?

    Yes. There is no pickup charge. You receive the rate card amount for your items, or you can choose to donate it.

    Do you take phones that don't switch on?

    Yes. Dead and broken items have their own rows on the rate card. A dead phone still contains copper, gold and a battery that must not go in the dustbin.

    What happens to my data?

    Tick “data wipe” while booking and it is recorded on your pickup and certificate. We still recommend you back up, log out of all accounts and factory-reset before handing over.

    Why should I share a code with the collector?

    The 4-digit code proves the collector actually met you. Without it the pickup cannot be closed, so nobody can mark your items as collected without you.

    Where do you operate?

    Mumbai, Thane and Navi Mumbai, Monday to Saturday between 9 am and 8 pm.

    Are Green Points the same as carbon credits?

    No. Carbon credits are a regulated market instrument. Green Points are EcoScrap reward points that you can spend in our rewards catalogue.

    Have a lot of e-waste? Talk to us.

    Offices, colleges and housing societies can schedule a bulk collection drive. Individuals can ask anything about a pickup.
