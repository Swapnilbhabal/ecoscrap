The toxic part of a useful object

Most of a device is harmless while it works. The trouble starts when it is broken open the wrong way, burnt for its copper or thrown in with household waste.

Lead

Solder on circuit boards, glass of old CRT TVs

Damages the nervous system and kidneys. Children are most at risk.

Mercury

Backlight lamps in older LCD screens, some switches

A neurotoxin that builds up in water and in the fish people eat.

Cadmium

Nickel-cadmium rechargeable batteries, old CRT coatings

Harms kidneys and bones, and is classed as a human carcinogen.

Brominated flame retardants

Plastic casings and circuit boards

When burnt in the open, they release dioxins and furans.

Lithium-ion cells

Phones, laptops, power banks

Swell, catch fire or explode when crushed in garbage trucks and dumps.

PVC insulation

Cables and wires

Burnt to reach the copper inside, it gives off acidic, toxic smoke.

Refrigerant gases

Fridges and air conditioners

Some deplete the ozone layer; many are strong greenhouse gases if vented.

None of this is a risk when it's handled properly

Authorised dismantlers remove batteries, capture gases and send boards to metal refiners. That's where every EcoScrap pickup goes.

India's E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022

Notified on 2 November 2022 and in force from 1 April 2023, the rules make the companies that sell electronics responsible for getting them recycled.

  • Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR). Every producer has a yearly recycling target based on what it sold.
  • EPR certificates. Registered recyclers generate certificates for the e-waste they recycle. Producers buy them to meet their targets.
  • Registration on the CPCB portal for manufacturers, producers, refurbishers and recyclers. Dealing with unregistered players is not allowed.
  • Environmental compensation can be charged for breaking the rules.
  • Solar panels now count as e-waste too.

Our recycler list comes from MPCB's authorisation list. Under the 2022 rules, check each recycler's registration on the CPCB EPR portal before a real launch.

Before you hand over a device

Five minutes now protects your data and your accounts.

  1. Back up photos, contacts and files.
  2. Sign out of Google or Apple ID, WhatsApp, UPI and banking apps. This also removes activation locks.
  3. Remove the SIM card and any memory card.
  4. Factory-reset phones and laptops. On laptops, wipe or remove the hard drive.

Don't take out swollen batteries yourself. Tell the collector instead.

How we estimate CO₂e avoided

Recycling metals and plastics avoids part of the emissions of mining and making new material. We show a simple, transparent estimate, not a certified carbon figure.
