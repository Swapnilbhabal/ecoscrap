The rate card

Every customer gets the same price for the same item in the same condition. No bargaining at the door, and no surprises.

ItemWorkingNot workingBroken / parts missingTypical weight

Amounts are per item, paid at pickup. Rates are set for this project demo and would be revised monthly from recycler buy-back prices.

Checked in front of you

The collector checks each item's condition at your door. If it differs from what you booked, the price moves to the matching row of this table. You see the change before you share your handover code.

Weighed in front of you

Items are weighed on a digital scale. The verified weight decides your Green Points and the CO₂ figure on your certificate.

Donate if you prefer

Tick “donate my payout” while booking and the amount goes to a tree-plantation drive. You still get the full Green Points.
